Short bio

Long bio

Jay McChord is a highly requested speaker, trainer, facilitator, and consultant to companies, institutions, and organizations globally. Recognized as an authority on the multi-generational workplace, his high-energy live and virtual events have earned him the nickname “Your Chief Encouragement Officer.”

Jay’s passion is to spark and empower teams to communicate differently and effectively in today’s eight-second world. He does this by teaching solid communication, leadership, and relationship skills that equip people and teams to thrive. He helps organizations lead, communicate, and sell across Traditionals, Baby Boomers, Gen-X, Millennials, and Gen-Z — in rooms that are live, virtual, and hybrid.

Jay brings a unique perspective as a former global senior sales trainer for a multi-billion dollar international technology company, an elected official, published author, and a military artist. Born in Lexington, Kentucky, he attended the University of Kentucky (Fine Art) and was the UK Wildcat mascot from 1988–1991. He later worked as an agent and registered representative for New York Life, then as a higher education development professional and marketing and government affairs director. After a decade of doing that work for others, he created McChord Inc. He served four terms on Lexington City Council and helped build the Legacy Trail and the Second Sunday health initiative.

Jay and his wife Jennifer have two children, Riley and Davis, and make their home in Lexington, KY.
