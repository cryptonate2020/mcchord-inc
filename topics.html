Relevant Leadership: Knowing the Times & Knowing What to Do

There are now three unique headwinds challenging your success:

  • More generations working side by side than ever before (up to five in some organizations).
  • People have the shortest attention spans in history (8 seconds… or less!).
  • Hybrid and AI-era chaos: live, virtual, and mixed rooms, with less human connection built in.

Each headwind on its own would challenge any leader. Together they create dynamics most teams have never had to manage at once.

This session equips emerging and existing leaders to communicate more concisely across all five generations — and to stay relevant when attention is short and work is hybrid. If you want to know the times and know what to do, this session is for you.

Professionalism in the Virtual Environment

Hybrid and remote work is now standard. Many professionals learned the buttons — how to host a meeting, record, and share a screen — but not how to be a professional inside a virtual or hybrid room.

Because of that gap, people still make mistakes that cost them and their organizations attention, trust, and deals.

This session focuses on the characteristics and practices of being a professional inside the virtual environment:

  • How to maintain your audience’s attention.
  • How to stay connected when talking one on one.
  • Appropriate attire guidelines so as to never send the wrong message.
  • Length of meetings.
  • When to start and when to finish.
  • And much more.

If you and/or your team do any work virtually, this session is a must.
